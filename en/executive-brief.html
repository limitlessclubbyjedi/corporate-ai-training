PROGRAMME & DELIVERABLES

THE WORKSHOP

One Day: From a Real Task
to a Working Prototype

Content and activities are tailored to your industry, departments, and participants' experience levels.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Understand AI's Role in Real Work

Move from AI assistants to agents and select tasks with clear inputs and outputs.

02 / DESIGN

Choose a Priority Use Case and Design an Agent

Define its role, business context, instructions, and acceptance criteria.

03 / BUILD & REVIEW

Build, Test, and Review

Each group tests at least one priority agent or reusable workflow, checking accuracy and appropriate use of information.

04 / ADOPT

Set Standards for Continued Adoption

Define human approvals, document reusable workflows, and establish a measurement baseline.

What Your Team Takes Away

Agent Role Map

Identify tasks and roles suited to AI.

Workflow Prototype

A starting point tested hands-on during the workshop.

Context & Quality Standard

Instructions, acceptance criteria, and human review checkpoints.

Measurement and Expansion Plan

Assign a workflow owner and define metrics for the trial.

The goal is to build the first workflow properly. Full implementation across departments and post-workshop support require a separately agreed scope.
