Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from Business Leaders

Do participants need prior AI experience?

The workshop can accommodate different experience levels. Participants should be comfortable using a computer and ready for a full day of hands-on work.

How many AI agents will we build in one day?

Each participant designs an Agent Role Map. Each group begins testing at least one priority agent or reusable workflow. The goal is to build the first one properly and create a foundation for more.

What does ten AI agents per employee mean?

It describes a team of agents developed over time around each person's responsibilities. It does not mean building all ten agents during the workshop.

Can the content be tailored to our business?

Examples, activities, and agent priorities are adapted to your industry, departments, and participants' experience levels.

Will we leave with a fully installed automation system?

The workshop develops your team's capabilities through agent role design and workflow testing. Full automation implementation is outside the scope of a one-day training programme.
